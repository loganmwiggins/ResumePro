# ResumePro
### Developed by Logan Wiggins, Margaret Rivas, and Vraj Patel 

## 📄 Overview
ResumePro is a powerful full-stack web application designed to help users craft professional, polished resumes with ease. our guided interface streamlines the resume-building process, ensuring that users can create high-quality resumes tailored to their needs. With customizable templates and AI-powered recommendations, ResumePro makes resume creation efficient, engaging, and stress-free. 

## 🏆 Features
-  **Multiple Resume Templates**: Choose from a variety of professionally designed templates suited for different industries.
-  **Intuitive Guided Input**: A seamless, step-by-step interface ensures users can easily enter their details without hassle.
-  **Live Preview**: See real-time updates to your resume as you fill in your information.
-  **User Accounts & Resume Management**: Register and log in to save, edit, and manage your resumes for future use.
-  **Export Options**: Download your resume as a PDF or Word document with a single click.
-  **AI-Assisted Optimization**: Leverage AI-driven recommendations to refine and enhance your resume content.

## 💻 Tech Stack
- **Front-end**: React.js
- **Back-end**: ASP.NET Core
- **Database**: Entity Framework Core
- **Additional Services**: OpenAI API (for AI-powered recommendations)
